Seedwell Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Seedwell is a single-player game of Ayo. This page explains what Seedwell does, and does not do, with information about you. The short version: Seedwell collects nothing.

Nothing leaves your phone

Seedwell has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics. It does not ask for your name, email, contacts, location, camera, microphone or any other personal information, and it does not use the internet at all.

What Seedwell keeps on your device

To remember your settings between games, Seedwell stores three small values in your phone's app storage: whether music is on, whether sound effects are on, and your longest winning run. These never leave your device. Uninstalling Seedwell, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

Permissions

Seedwell does not request any Android permissions.

Children

Seedwell is suitable for all ages. Because Seedwell collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Third parties

Seedwell contains no third-party advertising, tracking or analytics services, so no such service receives any information through Seedwell.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. A change would not allow Seedwell to collect data without saying so here first.

Contact

Questions about Seedwell or this policy? Contact the Seedwell apps dev team, Abuja, Nigeria.
